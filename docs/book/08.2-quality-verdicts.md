# Deciding a quality verdict

`08.1-quality-harness.md` says how a quality number is produced and
the basis-tuple quoting rule (numbers live in the private run ledger) how one is quoted. This page says how an arm is
**decided**: SHIP, REJECT, or UNRESOLVED.

**UNRESOLVED is a verdict, and it is the common one.** An arm whose delta is
smaller than the instrument's own noise floor has not been shipped and has not
been rejected -- it has not been measured. Recording it as either is the error
this page exists to prevent, and it is an error in both directions: a bare
threshold ("+0.3 ppl is a REJECT") means nothing when the null moves the scorer
by more than that.

---

## 1. The basis an arm is compared against

An arm is compared against **the same stack minus that arm**, scored in the same
process where the checkpoint fits, never against a number carried in from
another session or another document.

- An arm proposed for the **shipped default** is compared against the
  **zero-env default**.
- An arm proposed for a **record stack** is compared against **that entire stack
  with only this arm removed**. A record stack's env list is part of its basis:
  dropping one export -- a split count, a submit chunk -- changes the basis, and
  the gap that appears is the missing export, not the arm.
- An arm with no stack -- a new kernel, a new family -- is compared against the
  **reference path it replaces**: the CPU reference, the bf16 path, the cuda
  path. Never against a different checkpoint.

The verdict names its basis with the full env list, or it is not a verdict.

## 2. The noise floor is measured per family, not assumed

Before any arm is adjudicated, the scorer's floor for that family is measured
with a **null**: a change that is provably the same arithmetic, through the
identical harness, checkpoint and corpus. A **split count** is the canonical
null -- stage1 partitions the same masked position set across a different number
of workgroups and stage2 merges the partials with the same online-softmax
rescaling, so every rung computes the same function in a different reduction
order.

### 2a. The null must perform the arm's own operation, not merely share its family

A reassociation null holds the quantized bytes fixed and only reorders
arithmetic. An arm that **requantizes** -- that changes which values share a
block scale, or what those values are -- is not bounded by it, and reading such
an arm against a reassociation floor overstates its effect without limit. The
floor and the arm must be the **same class of operation on the same family**;
per-family is necessary and not sufficient.

The measurement that establishes this: qwen3.8-27B's reassociation floor is
0.0022 single-cell, the tightest on record. An element-granular permutation
arm -- which requantizes -- read 3.0x that floor and would have shipped. Measured
instead against its own class of null, arbitrary permutations carrying no design
intent, the distribution is mean -0.0063, sd 0.0031, and the designed arm sits
**0.06 sd from the centre of it**. Two controls close it: a random permutation
scored a larger `|t|` than the designed arm, and a counter-arm built to be worse
landed on the same side of zero.

The corollary for an **inconclusive** verdict: before reading UNRESOLVED as a
statement about the arm, price the instrument with a **closed-form positive
control** -- a known, previously published effect of comparable size, reproduced
in the same session. If the control does not clear the bar either, the round has
measured the scorer's resolving power, not the arm, and the verdict line must
say so.

### 2b. Slices must be equal in size, and `|t| >= 2` alone is not evidence

**Every slice must cover the same token count.** A paired `t` is invariant to how
independent per-chunk deltas are grouped -- 4 slices, 20 and 580 all resolve the
same effect at `t` -2.68, -2.96 and -2.86 -- so resolution buys nothing. What
destroys power is a **ragged remainder slice** weighted as if it were full: a
grid of 580 chunks cut into slices of 32 leaves a 19th slice holding 4 chunks,
which biased the mean 35% low and inflated the sd 70%. Dropping that one slice
moved `t` from -1.34 to -3.58. Cut the corpus to a whole multiple of the slice
size, or weight each slice by its token count; never let a short tail vote as a
full slice.

**A finer instrument raises `|t|` on nulls too.** At 580 equal slices a random
permutation reaches `t` -2.50 against zero, clearing the bar while carrying no
design intent whatever. So `|t| >= 2` against the unmodified basis is necessary
and never sufficient: an arm must beat **its own null distribution**, not zero.
Where the two disagree, the null wins.

The reason is that the variance is in the checkpoint, not the scorer: per-chunk
sd is ~0.0134 against means of ~0.001, irreducible by slicing. Re-cutting the
same tokens cannot supply power; section 2c says what sets it, and the answer is
not simply "more tokens".

The suites that measure it are
`ppl_e4b::e4b_split_count_reassociation_ladder_and_top2_gap_density_bound_what_one_ppl_cell_resolves`
and its `ppl_gemma4_moe` twin. A family with more than one split-k arm has a
ladder per arm and the floor to quote is the one on the arm serving decodes:
gemma4-E4B's deep band is laddered by the first suite and its shallow merge
width, the one every zero-env decode uses, by
`ppl_e4b::e4b_shallow_split_count_reassociation_ladder_is_the_floor_on_the_path_serving_decodes`.
The in-process three-arm form that prices one
kernel swap against the null in a single process is
`ppl_gptoss::the_flash_sliding_kernel_swap_moves_ppl_less_than_a_pure_reassociation_of_the_same_default_does`.
The paired-slice form that turns one arm into a mean, a standard error, a `t` and
a sign count -- and prices the arm's own null over the same slices -- is
`ppl_gptoss::the_int8_attention_projection_arm_is_priced_over_paired_corpus_slices_against_the_bf16_basis_and_against_this_scorers_own_reassociation_null`.

**The floor is a property of the model and the task, not of the kernel**, and it
is amplified by the density of near-ties among the scored positions -- the
`near_tie_fraction` those suites print beside every rung. A reassociation
perturbs every logit by float noise; it changes the *argmax* only where the top-2
gap is smaller than that noise, and a flipped argmax on a scored position moves
that position's log-prob by whole nats. A family that puts a quarter of its
scored positions on a top-2 gap under 0.5 therefore has a floor in **whole ppl
units**; a family whose scored positions sit on wide margins has a floor in
**hundredths**. The density orders the families by risk, it does not predict the
floor to a digit -- so a high density means *measure the ladder*, and only the
ladder is the floor. Two families measured by the same harness on the same corpus
do not share a floor, and neither inherits the other's precedents.

**A null that moves top-1 accuracy is not a null.** Float noise flips only
near-ties, so a reduction-order change moves accuracy by a few tokens in 512 at
most. A rung whose accuracy collapses is a stage2 merging a different number of
partials than stage1 wrote -- a defect on the measurement knob, not a floor. Both
ladder suites assert this per rung
(`A_REASSOCIATION_NULL_MAY_NOT_MOVE_TOP1_ACCURACY_BY_MORE_THAN_THIS_OR_THE_SPLIT_COUNT_IS_NOT_THE_SAME_ARITHMETIC`),
which is what separates a measurable floor from a broken knob.

### 2c. The sample size is context windows, not tokens

A corpus of `T` tokens read at `n_ctx = D` is **`floor(T / D)` independent
samples, not `T`**. The paired delta is correlated across the whole of a window,
because every position in it shares one prefill and one KV history. Measured at
`D` 16384 on two arms and a null alike, the between-window variance of the window
means is **22.1x, 26.1x and 27.7x** what the within-window scatter predicts: so
147,438 scored positions carry the power of **18** samples, and `|t|` scales as
`sqrt(T / D)`.

This is what reconciles two results that read as contradictory and are not. A
**0.178%** effect resolves at `t` -2.86 at `n_ctx` 512, which is 580 windows; a
**2.0%** effect fails at `t` 1.22 at `n_ctx` 16384, which is 18 windows -- same
corpus, same checkpoint, same protocol. **A perplexity verdict names its `n_ctx`
or it is not a verdict**, and a verdict taken at one depth does not describe
another: on this family the ranking of arms inverts between 16384 and 131072.

On wikitext-2 test (297,193 tokens, 1290590 bytes, sha256 `173c87a5...`) at
`n_ctx` 16384 the minimum detectable effect is **1.5-1.9% of ppl at `|t| >= 2`
and 2.2-2.8% at `|t| >= 3`** -- a range, because it is a property of the noise
and the three contrasts measured there carry per-window sd 0.0314 to 0.0392.
Round it up and act on it: **a sub-3% perplexity difference on this corpus at
serving depth is UNRESOLVED, whatever its sign and however many slices it is cut
into.**

### 2d. More corpus raises `|t|` and never separates an arm from its null

Arm and null both scale as `sqrt(windows)`, so **their ratio is
corpus-invariant**. On the cross-engine contrast that ratio is **1.55x**: the
corpus at which the arm first reaches `|t|` 2 -- 2.6x wikitext-2 test -- puts the
null at 1.29, and the corpus that takes the arm to 3 also carries the null past
2. There is no corpus size at which perplexity separates that pair.

So scoring more tokens buys a number, not a verdict. It also costs one: the
moment the corpus changes, every published cell measured on the old corpus stops
being comparable and is re-opened, not extended. Before proposing corpus as the
lever, state the arm-to-null ratio it has to beat -- and if that ratio is near
1, the instrument is wrong for the contrast and section 4 says which one is not.

## 3. Acceptance, by class of change

| class | what it claims | what decides it |
|---|---|---|
| **bitwise** | computes the identical function in an order proven to produce identical bits | logits bit-for-bit against the basis on a tiny-shape suite, plus a per-arm engagement assert. A ppl cell adds nothing and any ppl move at all re-classes the arm |
| **fp-reorder** | same function, different reduction order or fused pass | the paired delta must sit **inside** the family's measured floor. The arm *is* a null; being indistinguishable from one is the pass condition |
| **quantization** | changes the function on purpose | the delta must first be **resolvable** from the floor, and only then read for sign |

Concretely:

- A **bitwise** arm that moves the scorer is not bitwise. Fix the claim or fix
  the kernel; do not price it.
- An **fp-reorder** arm cannot be rejected by a single cell whose delta is inside
  the floor, however large that delta looks. Two kernels that derive the same
  window, carry the sink the same way and differ only in reduction order are
  adjudicated against the null, not against a bar.
- A **quantization** arm is resolvable when either the paired mean over **nine or
  more independent context windows** reaches `|t| >= 2`, or a single-cell delta
  exceeds the family's measured floor. Windows, not slices: nine slices cut from
  one context window are one sample, per section 2c, and a corpus that yields
  fewer than nine windows at the depth in question cannot adjudicate this class at
  that depth at all. Resolvable and held-or-better ships;
  resolvable and worse is a REJECT at any magnitude, because a family whose floor
  is in the hundredths can genuinely resolve a tenth of a ppl. Not resolvable is
  UNRESOLVED.

**What UNRESOLVED forbids.** An UNRESOLVED arm changes no default and earns no
claim. It may still ride a record stack if it buys measured wall time, but
nothing in the tree may name it as quality-preserving or quality-costing, and a
constant whose name states a ppl magnitude for it is asserting something that was
never measured. Restate the constant in terms of what *was* established -- the
form that shipped, and against which basis -- or re-measure it with a paired
method.

**A sign that flips between configurations rejects on its own.** An arm whose
delta reverses sign across split counts, corpus slices, **depths** or stacks is
not a quality cost in either direction, and this holds regardless of magnitude.
That inference survives a large floor; the magnitudes attached to it do not, and
must not be quoted as the arm's cost. Depth is the one that catches cross-engine
and cross-quant rows: on qwen3.8 the ours-vs-Q4_K_M gap reads -0.10% at `n_ctx`
16384 and +0.72% at 131072, so no single-depth cell of it is a verdict.

## 4. Accuracy and the shuffled control: one gates, one informs, one may decide

- The **shuffled control gates the run, not the arm**. `ppl_common`'s
  `print_machine_line_with_acc_and_assert_real_beats_shuffled` asserts real beats
  shuffled by orders of magnitude; that proves the harness scored the real
  sequence through a live model. A control that is not orders of magnitude worse
  voids the row.
- **Top-1 accuracy informs, at the resolution its position count supports.** On a
  512-position cell its quantum is 0.002 and a delta under 0.01 is one to five
  tokens: it gates nothing there and no arm is rejected on it. On a
  147,438-position corpus cell the quantum is 6.8e-6, its own null is measurable,
  and it is **roughly twenty times the more sensitive instrument**. Measured on
  identical positions of identical runs at `n_ctx` 16384, perplexity separates arm
  from null by **1.55-2.11x** where accuracy separates by **33-42x**, and
  accuracy's between-window variance inflation is **1.7-3.1x** against
  perplexity's 22-28x. That gap is structural, not a draw of the dice: a bounded
  0/1 per-position statistic cannot be dragged by a handful of catastrophic
  log-probs, and a mean log-prob can.

**Accuracy may decide a contrast perplexity leaves UNRESOLVED**, and only then,
when all three hold:

1. the accuracy delta beats **its own measured null on the same corpus** by the
   same `|t| >= 2` the perplexity verdict would have had to clear;
2. the **per-window sign count is consistent** -- the distribution-free form, and
   the clause a null fails: 18 windows to 0 on an arm against 9 to 8 on a null is
   `p` 7.6e-6 with no variance model at all;
3. the delta exceeds the accuracy MDE measured on that corpus (0.13-0.16
   accuracy points at `|t| >= 2` on wikitext-2 test at `n_ctx` 16384).

Below that bar accuracy still only informs. **The separate, higher bar is what
keeps the promotion from being circular**: accuracy also defines what counts as a
null, via section 2's
`A_REASSOCIATION_NULL_MAY_NOT_MOVE_TOP1_ACCURACY_BY_MORE_THAN_THIS_OR_THE_SPLIT_COUNT_IS_NOT_THE_SAME_ARITHMETIC`,
and a statistic that both defines the reference and scores against it decides
nothing unless the two thresholds are stated apart. Two limits ride with it:
accuracy is argmax-only and blind to calibration, so a temperature-sampled serving
path is not fully described by it; and **an accuracy gap between two engines
running bit-identical weights owes a mechanism before it is quoted as a quality
win**, because a harness asymmetry produces the same reading.

## 5. When ppl and accuracy disagree in sign

They disagree exactly where the arm moves near-tie positions, which on an
amplified family is most of the time. Neither instrument overturns the other by
being smaller-grained, and disagreement is not a tiebreak -- it is a demand for a
different instrument:

- both deltas inside their floors -> **UNRESOLVED**;
- ppl resolvable, accuracy disagreeing -> decide on a **real-weights greedy
  continuation**: run the same prompt under both arms for at least 128 steps and
  compare token by token. Token-identical means the arm changes no observable
  output and ships; a divergence makes it a real change and the ppl sign decides;
- **ppl UNRESOLVED, accuracy clearing section 4's three-clause bar** -> the
  accuracy sign decides, and the verdict records the ppl as UNRESOLVED beside it.
  This is the case section 4 exists for, and it is the common one at serving
  depth, where a corpus yields tens of windows rather than hundreds.

A greedy continuation is also what settles a REJECT that would otherwise rest on
a ppl delta inside the MDE: a divergence proves the arm is a real change, but the
direction still has to come from an instrument that resolves, so an
inside-the-MDE ppl sign cannot supply it.

## 6. When a real-weights argmax A/B replaces the ppl cell

The ppl cell scores one prompt, at one depth, on one split count. It is **blind
to any arm that engages only past a knee it never reaches** -- a depth-gated
split band, a chunked-prefill route, a ring wrap, a batch scheduler. Such an arm
returns a digit-identical cell, and a digit-identical cell is then evidence about
the gate, not about the arm.

An arm in that shape is adjudicated one of two ways, never by the plain cell:

1. **Lower the knee** with the band's own env override so the band's arithmetic
   runs on real weights over the scored range, and price the band there; or
2. **Argmax A/B at a depth where the gate fires**: the same greedy continuation
   under both arms, compared token by token.

Either way, **engagement is proved on every row** -- a changed pass count, a
changed `weight_bytes_per_token`, a printed split count, or a per-position
difference from the basis. A row with no engagement proof is void, and a
digit-identical row with no structural reason for identity is an arm that never
took.

## 7. The verdict line

A verdict states, in one place: the arm, its class, its **basis with the full env
list**, the **corpus and the `n_ctx` it was scored at** (which is what sets the
sample size, per section 2c), the **method** (single cell / split-ladder mean /
paired windows with `t` and sign counts / in-process A/B), the **family's own
measured floor**, the delta **with its uncertainty**, the engagement proof, and
the log path. A verdict missing the floor is a threshold applied to an unmeasured
instrument, and reads as evidence while being none.

**A threshold must be a null's spread, and it must be this family's.** Two ways
of getting that wrong have both shipped here and both read as rigour:

- **An arm delta used as a floor.** A floor is how far the scorer moves when the
  arithmetic did not, so a number that is some *arm's* measured effect cannot
  bound another arm. It also scales the wrong way: the worse the reference arm,
  the larger the delta it licenses.
- **Another family's number used as a floor.** Section 2's rule that families do
  not share floors is not advisory. A qwen3.8 wikitext-2 figure quoted as the
  rejection bar for a gemma4 chat-wrapped 512-position cell crosses the family,
  the corpus, the harness and the scored-position count in one step, and the
  multiplier it produces ("7.7x the floor") is arithmetic on two unrelated
  quantities.

A constant whose **name** states such a threshold propagates it into every
assertion message that cites it, and those outlive the round that measured it.
Name a threshold for the null it came from, or do not name it at all.
