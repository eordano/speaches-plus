BASIS

Every pin uses the same numerator: ≈20.5 GB weights-resident read per token (qwen3.8-27B at 4-bit), divided by that engine's measured decode ms/token at depth 256. Same model, same card, same formula — apples to apples. vLLM's decode rows are still being measured; its pin is the llama.cpp decode position scaled by the measured prefill ratio (2404 / 2569) and drawn dashed until real rows land.

PAST THE WALL

The bus cannot move more than 1.47 TB/s, and no single-token decoder can beat that. MTP speculative decode emits more than one accepted token per weight pass, so the per-token effective read rate lands at 1.67 TB/s — past the physical wall. You don't break physics; you amortize it.
